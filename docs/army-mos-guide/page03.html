Training Reality — Verified vs. Open-Source Claims
AIT attrition numbers circulate widely online. Here's what independently verified against primary sources vs. what's forum lore.

🟢 17C / Cyber School — Fort Eisenhower Solid

  • Renamed from Fort Gordon Oct 27, 2023 — current.
  • Oct 2024 & Feb 2025: ARCYBER/USCYBERCOM approved 17C AIT + 170A WOBC as "Alternate Training Solutions" for DoD 8140 Host/Network Analyst certs — a real, dateable pipeline streamlining.
  • Two-phase structure (foundational IT → offensive/defensive methods) confirmed.

🟠 Attrition Figures Unconfirmed

  • "JCAC 85% peak attrition" (old Corry Station model) — widely repeated, not traced to a Navy/DTIC document.
  • "<5% current Fort Eisenhower attrition" and "4 failures of 14 tests" rule — no official source found; plausible direction, unconfirmed specifics.
  • AIT week-counts (17C: 36 vs ~45wk; 35N: 24 vs up to 52wk incl. NSA cert) — contested across sources, verify with a recruiter before planning around them.

Bottom Line for a Candidate with Foreign-National Parents

The cognitive bar (ASVAB, ICTL) and training rigor are real but surmountable. The actual hurdle is the Tier 5 / Guideline B process — treat it as a documentation and disclosure strategy, not an afterthought.

Best for computer science strength17C — highest post-service ROI, fastest-growing DoD domain, but the longest/most contested AIT pipeline.
Best for analytical/briefing strength35F — broadest exposure across the intel community, shortest AIT.
Best for spatial/signal strength35G / 35N — deep technical specialization, verify current training site/length with a recruiter.
Non-negotiable prep, any MOSFinancial independence from foreign parents, full SF-86 disclosure, resolve dual-citizenship/passport status before enlistment.
